# 🍵 Artisan Matcha

### A Cinematic Scrollytelling Experience for a Luxury Iced Matcha Latte

A premium animation-driven landing page built with **Next.js 14**, **TypeScript**, **Tailwind CSS**, **Framer Motion**, and a custom **scroll-linked image sequence renderer**.

Artisan Matcha explores how motion, typography, and visual storytelling can transform a simple product into an immersive digital experience. The project focuses on creating a cinematic product reveal through smooth scroll interactions, high-end visuals, and luxury-inspired design principles.

## 🎬 Core Experience

The centerpiece of the project is a custom scroll-driven image sequence animation.

As the user scrolls:

1. A pristine iced matcha latte is revealed.
2. Ice cubes begin lifting from the cup.
3. Matcha and cream erupt into motion.
4. Liquid droplets suspend in space.
5. The product transforms into an exploded composition.
6. Supporting sections continue the visual narrative.

The experience is designed to feel cinematic, immersive, and product-focused from the very first viewport.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

Artisan Matcha explores the intersection of product visualization, motion design, and interactive storytelling.

The experience was built around three core principles:

### Motion First

Movement is treated as a primary design element. Scroll interactions, transitions, and animations guide attention and reveal the product progressively.

### Product as the Hero

The animated matcha composition remains the focal point of the experience, driving the narrative from the first scroll to the final section.

### Cinematic Storytelling

Rather than functioning as a traditional marketing website, the project is structured as a visual journey that combines animation, typography, and composition to create an immersive experience.
